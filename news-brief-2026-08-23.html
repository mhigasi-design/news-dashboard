US-Canada trade war escalates as talks collapse, 50% tariffs take effect
Trade negotiations broke down Friday night; Trump's 50% tariffs on roughly $20B of Canadian goods (hockey sticks, wine, cement, dairy, and more) took effect at midnight. US envoy: Israel gave no warning before Syria strike that risked clash with Turkey
Tom Barrack, US ambassador to Turkey and special envoy to Syria, said Israel gave no warning to Washington or Ankara ahead of Tuesday's strike on Syria's Abu Duhur air base, calling it a possible attempt to "bait" Turkey. Israel's defense minister disputed the no-warning claim.

Global Health AP · Al Jazeera
DR Congo receives first Ebola vaccine shipment as outbreak outpaces historic epidemic's rate
16,250 doses of the Ervebo vaccine arrived in Kinshasa, part of a 70,000-dose WHO allocation to be trialed against the rare Bundibugyo strain. The outbreak has already killed 2,557 of 5,375 confirmed cases and is on track to surpass the deadliest Ebola outbreak on record.
