Conflict BBC · Al Jazeera
Massive Russian strike kills at least 9, cuts power to ~600,000 in Kyiv area
Russia struck Ukrainian energy and communications infrastructure overnight, killing at least nine and leaving hundreds of thousands without power — days after Trump's disputed "energy ceasefire" claim. Zelensky says allies failed to give early warning.
Middle East BBC · Guardian
Two container ships sunk/struck near the Strait of Hormuz
The Singapore-flagged GFS Pride sank after being hit by a projectile Tuesday; a second vessel, GFS Giselle, was struck Wednesday and limped to anchorage. The Guardian reports it's the sharpest rise in Iranian attacks on shipping since the US-Israel-Iran war.
Geopolitics BBC · SCMP · Al Jazeera
Musk: US government, not SpaceX, controls Starlink/Starshield limits
Musk responded after Ukraine reported unexplained new speed restrictions (capped ~100mph) disrupting drone and air-defense operations since Tuesday night. A Zelensky adviser called the timing no coincidence given US-Kyiv strains.
Legal BBC · SCMP · Guardian
Nord Stream sabotage trial opens in Hamburg
Serhii Kuznetsov, a Ukrainian ex-military officer, went on trial accused of leading the diver team that destroyed the Nord Stream pipelines in 2022. Prosecutors call it a war crime; the defence argues Russia used the pipelines as a "weapon." Trial expected to run into February.
Policy BBC · Guardian
Violent student protests erupt in Tirana, Albania
Tens of thousands of students protesting education costs and corruption set fire to a government ministry building and two banks; police used water cannon and tear gas — the most violent unrest yet against PM Edi Rama.
