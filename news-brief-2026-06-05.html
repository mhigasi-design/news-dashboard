Lebanon Al Jazeera · UN News
Hezbollah dismisses ceasefire deal as "surrender" — Israeli strikes continue
Israel and Lebanon agreed a conditional ceasefire framework in US-led talks, but Hezbollah chief Naim Qassem publicly rejected it, saying the deal amounted to "surrender." Israeli airstrikes continued; more than 3,500 killed in Lebanon since March. A Serbian UNIFIL peacekeeper was killed by mortar fire near Marjayoun — the 7th UN fatality.
Disarmament OPCW · UN News
OPCW finds undeclared Syrian chemical weapons — rockets match 2013 Ghouta massacre
Inspectors deployed to Syria in May 2026 found chemical munitions, related materials, and 60,000+ pages of documents — including rockets matching those used in the 2013 Ghouta attack that killed hundreds. The new government under President al-Sharaa granted full access. UN disarmament chief: "a momentous discovery for international security and the global disarmament regime."
Ukraine Al Jazeera
Russia kills 12 across Ukraine — Zelensky marks 707 children killed since 2022
Russian bomb and drone attacks struck Donetsk, Kharkiv, and Sumy regions, killing at least 12 and wounding scores. On the International Day of Innocent Children Victims of Aggression, Zelensky said Russia has killed 707 children since February 2022 and wounded or abducted thousands more.
US Politics Al Jazeera
John Bolton pleads guilty to illegal document retention — $2M+ fine
Former National Security Adviser John Bolton pleaded guilty to one count of illegally retaining sensitive national security documents. He had been indicted on 18 Espionage Act counts in October 2025, including allegations he shared 1,000+ pages of classified diary-like entries. Bolton, a vocal Trump critic since his 2020 memoir, is the first Trump-era NSA to plead in a documents case.
Middle East Al Jazeera
Israeli Supreme Court orders Red Cross access restored for 9,000 Palestinian prisoners
Israel's Supreme Court unanimously ruled that the government must repeal its ban on ICRC visits to Palestinian detainees, calling the 3-year-old policy illegal under Israeli and international law. The ban had been in place since the October 2023 Hamas attack — the first time in 50 years Israel barred Red Cross access. Around 9,000 Palestinian security prisoners will be eligible for visits.
Global Health Al Jazeera · UN News
Kenya's High Court halts US Ebola treatment facility at Laikipia Air Base
Kenya's High Court suspended construction of a US military Ebola quarantine facility at Laikipia Air Base for three weeks, after nationwide protests from healthcare workers. The facility would treat American citizens infected abroad to prevent their repatriation. Kenya has never recorded Ebola; medics say: "If it's too dangerous for America, it's too dangerous for Kenya." President Ruto defended the plan as part of a longstanding US-Kenya partnership.
