US Policy Al Jazeera · CNN · Bloomberg · SCMP
Supreme Court (6–3) upholds birthright citizenship — major blow to Trump's immigration agenda
Chief Justice Roberts joined Barrett and all three liberal justices to strike down Trump's executive order curbing 14th Amendment citizenship. The order would have created an estimated 255,000 stateless children annually. Trump called the ruling "too bad for our country" and is now appealing to Congress.
Middle East Al Jazeera · CNN
Iran-US Doha talks begin — Tehran rejects third-party Hormuz mine clearance; MoU at breaking point
US envoys met Iran's technical delegation indirectly in Qatar Tuesday after IRGC ballistic missile strikes on Kuwait and Bahrain (Day 121). Tehran insists Hormuz mine clearance is governed solely by its own MoU interpretation, rejecting any external role. Iran FM denies "direct US negotiations" are planned.
Disaster Al Jazeera · CNN
Venezuela earthquake death toll surpasses 1,700 — USGS warns final count could reach 10,000
Six days after the June 24–25 twin earthquakes (7.2 and 7.5M) near La Guaira and Yumare, 1,719 are confirmed dead and tens of thousands remain missing. A Jordanian rescue team pulled a toddler alive from rubble. Officials say rescues are likely transitioning to recovery operations.
Climate Al Jazeera · CNN
European heatwave kills 1,300+ — Italy 22 cities on red alert; new surge forecast July 5–6
WHO reports 1,300 excess heat deaths across Europe since June 21. France alone has recorded 1,000 deaths, with Paris funeral services overwhelmed. Italy placed 22 cities on red alert; Croatia's Adriatic island of Vis battles wildfires. Temperatures 8–10°C above seasonal average. Forecasters warn of a second, more intense heat dome arriving July 5–6.
Weather Al Jazeera · SCMP
Tropical Storm Mekkhala floods Taiwan, sweeps toward Japan — 100+ flights cancelled
Tropical Storm Mekkhala left parts of Taiwan severely flooded before tracking northeast toward Japan. Packing gusts of up to 144 km/h, it has triggered factory shutdowns, evacuations in Kyushu, and the cancellation of over 100 flights across Japan. A second storm, Higos, is also approaching the region.
