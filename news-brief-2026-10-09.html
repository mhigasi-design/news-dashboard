Conflict BBC · Al Jazeera
Russian strike on buses kills at least 30 as deadly attacks on Ukraine surge
A Russian glide bomb hit two civilian buses in the front-line city of Kramatorsk, killing at least 30 with 18 wounded; 3 more died in nearby Bilenke. Officials halted public transport in Kramatorsk and Sloviansk. It follows a strike that killed 22, including 5 children, in Pryluky a day earlier.
Middle East BBC · Al Jazeera
Three killed in Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia airports over two days
Houthi missiles and drones hit Abha and Riyadh's King Khalid airports on Oct 6–7, killing 3 and wounding 36. The Iran-backed group says the strikes answer Saudi airstrikes on Yemen after Houthi forces seized the Red Sea port of Mokha, escalating the renewed war.
Legal BBC · Al Jazeera
Venezuela's Maduro charged with conspiracy to commit torture
A superseding indictment accuses Maduro and wife Cilia Flores of torturing detainees, including three US citizens, with electric shocks, asphyxiation and rape to "entrench themselves in power." Both deny the charges, added to existing narco-terrorism counts; trial begins June 2027.
Policy / Tech BBC · Al Jazeera
White House blocks Microsoft from foreign worker hiring programme
VP Vance barred Microsoft, Adobe and six IT outsourcers from the green-card PERM pathway, alleging H-1B fraud and accusing Microsoft of replacing laid-off Americans with "foreign indentured servants." Microsoft says 80% of its H-1B filings extend existing staff, paid at market rates.
