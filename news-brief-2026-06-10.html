📰 Top Stories 5
US–Iran Al Jazeera · SCMP
US retaliates after Iran downs Apache helicopter over Strait of Hormuz
Iran shot down a US Apache helicopter over Hormuz on June 9; both pilots were rescued near Oman unharmed. Trump authorized CENTCOM "self-defence strikes" on Sirik port and surrounding Hormuz sites. Iran's FM demanded US military withdrawal, warning: "Our powerful armed forces will leave no attack unanswered." Trump says a deal is "pretty close."
Middle East Al Jazeera · SCMP
Trump warns Netanyahu "you'll be on your own" as Israel kills 17 in Lebanon
Even as Trump claimed the Iran deal was in "final throes," Israel struck Tyre and surrounding Lebanese towns on June 9, killing at least 17. Israel has conducted nearly 3,500 air attacks since an April ceasefire. Trump urged Netanyahu to "be careful," but Israel says it will continue targeting Hezbollah regardless. Overall death toll in Lebanon since March 2: 3,666.
Natural Disaster SCMP
Philippines 7.8-magnitude earthquake kills 37, injures 400+ in Mindanao
A magnitude 7.8 quake struck 20km off Sarangani province on Monday morning, collapsing a commercial building in General Santos City. Tremors were felt as far as Manado, Indonesia (260+ miles). Tsunami warnings were issued across multiple countries. Four remain missing; rescue operations are ongoing.
South Asia Al Jazeera
11 killed in Pakistan-administered Kashmir as clashes erupt over election seats
Police and protesters clashed in Rawalakot on June 8 over 12 reserved legislative seats designated for refugees from Indian-administered Kashmir. The JAAC demands all 45 seats be open to current residents. Authorities banned the group under anti-terrorism laws and deployed federal paramilitary forces. July 27 elections loom.
World Cup 2026 Al Jazeera
FIFA World Cup 2026 opening ceremonies begin tomorrow across three nations
The first-ever tri-nation World Cup opens Thursday: Estadio Azteca, Mexico City (June 11) followed by Toronto and LA (June 12). Performers include Shakira, J Balvin and Burna Boy (Mexico), Michael Bublé and Alanis Morissette (Canada), and Katy Perry and Future (US). ~200,000 combined live spectators; global audience in the hundreds of millions.
