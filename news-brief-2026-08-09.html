US Policy BBC · CNN
Todd Blanche narrowly confirmed as US attorney general
Trump's former personal lawyer won confirmation 50–49 overnight, with Republicans Susan Collins and Lisa Murkowski joining all Democrats in opposition. Sen. Bill Cassidy cast the deciding vote after Blanche pledged to walk back a disputed $1.8bn "anti-weaponisation" fund. Blanche had served as acting AG since April.
Latin America AP · Al Jazeera
Car bomb hits southwest Colombia a day after new president's inauguration
The army says two FARC dissident groups carried out the toll-plaza bombing on the Pan-American Highway, a day after right-wing lawyer Abelardo de la Espriella — who replaces leftist Gustavo Petro — was sworn in in Cali on promises to crack down on armed groups. Two security guards suffered minor injuries.
Climate Al Jazeera · BBC
Fast-moving Bald Range wildfire doubles in size, forces mass evacuations in B.C.
More than 20,000 people were ordered out overnight as the Bald Range fire, first spotted Friday evening, grew to 95 sq km by Saturday morning near Summerland and Peachland. Premier David Eby declared a provincewide state of emergency as crews battle dozens of out-of-control fires across British Columbia.
Climate BBC · Al Jazeera
Typhoon Dolphin batters Japan's Okinawa before bearing down on China
The "very strong" typhoon cut power to at least 44,000 buildings and injured five people in Okinawa, with winds of 162km/h. It's expected to make landfall on China's eastern coast late Sunday or early Monday between Zhejiang and Fujian; Zhejiang has raised its typhoon alert to the highest level and suspended 162 ferry routes.
