Israel/Lebanon Al Jazeera
Israeli forces cross Litani River, encircle Nabatieh — first breach of buffer zone since 2006
Israel's military crossed Lebanon's Litani River and moved on Nabatieh, a key southern economic and cultural hub. Over 1.2 million Lebanese are now displaced — more than 20% of the population. Lebanese PM Nawaf Salam called the advance "dangerous and unprecedented." Israeli and Lebanese military officials held direct talks at the Pentagon the same day.
Indo-Pacific SCMP · Al Jazeera
Hegseth at Shangri-La: Allies must spend 3.5% GDP; China's expansion threatens regional balance
At Asia's premier security forum in Singapore, US Defense Secretary Hegseth demanded allies end "free-riding," warned Beijing's military expansion could unravel the Indo-Pacific balance of power, and reaffirmed US commitment to Taiwan. First major US defense posture signal since the Trump-Xi summit. The US will take a "strong, quiet, clear" approach to alliances.
Latin America AS/COA · Congress.gov
Colombia votes today — post-Petro presidential race too close to call; runoff to June 21 expected
Colombia holds its first-round presidential election today. Incumbent Gustavo Petro is barred from a second term. Leftist senator Iván Cepeda (Pacto Histórico) leads polls at ~44%, with independent Abelardo de la Espriella at ~22% and Centro Democrático's Paloma Valencia at ~20%. A candidate needs 50%+ to avoid a June 21 runoff — widely expected.
