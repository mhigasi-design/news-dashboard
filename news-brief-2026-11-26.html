Trade Policy BBC · Al Jazeera
US announces new tariffs on host of countries including China
Washington unveiled fresh duties hitting China (up to 40%), the EU, Vietnam (up to 35%) and 11 other trading partners, following the Supreme Court's ruling that Trump's earlier "Liberation Day" tariffs were illegal. The new rates aren't expected to take effect until next year; the EU says it will investigate.
Middle East BBC · Al Jazeera
Israeli judges recommend dropping most serious charges against Netanyahu
A Jerusalem court panel told Israel's attorney general there is "little chance" of conviction in Netanyahu's lead corruption case and urged a "rethink" of the other two. Netanyahu, six years into the trial, called the cases "fabricated" and demanded they end immediately — a boost as rival Gadi Eisenkot tries to form a government.
Gaza BBC · Reuters
Israeli strike kills senior Hamas security official and family in Gaza City
Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, 47, head of Hamas's internal security service in northern Gaza, was killed with his wife and two teenage daughters when their Shati refugee camp apartment was bombed at dawn. Israel's military called him a "significant figure"; it is the second killing of a senior Hamas security official in 24 hours, despite the ceasefire in place since October last year.
Ukraine War BBC · Bloomberg
Five Russian military jets hit in airfield strike, says Ukraine
Kyiv says it damaged three MiG-31s and two Su-34s at Russia's Savasleyka airfield, 300km east of Moscow, while also striking two oil refineries in Russia's Samara region overnight. Putin says Moscow will "inevitably" retaliate; ten people were killed across Ukraine in Russian strikes over the same 24 hours.
