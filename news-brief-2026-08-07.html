📰 Top Stories 4
Geopolitics BBC · Al Jazeera
Trump denies US munitions shortage, vows to jail "leakers"
Trump posted that the US has "massive amounts" of munitions and more being manufactured, after mounting questions about the state of the military's arsenal six months into the Iran war. Iran says its own production gives it the ability to respond to "any threat."
Conflict BBC · Al Jazeera
Houthi attacks reportedly kill at least 30 Yemeni government forces
Ballistic missiles and drones struck military camps in Yemen's Marib and Hadramawt provinces. The Iran-backed Houthis said they targeted "large Saudi military build-ups" preparing an offensive; Yemen's defence ministry called the attacks "treacherous."
US Policy AP · Al Jazeera
Trump signs new executive orders on birthright citizenship
Two immigration actions target the number of people born in the US who can become citizens and "birth tourism." The order is narrower than Trump's first attempt, which the Supreme Court rejected in June while upholding a broad reading of birthright citizenship.
Middle East BBC · Al Jazeera
Israel charges settler over killing of Palestinian activist
Yinon Levy was charged with reckless manslaughter over the July 2025 shooting of Odeh Hathaleen, who helped film the Oscar-winning documentary "No Other Land." Rights group B'Tselem says it's the first such indictment in nearly three years.
