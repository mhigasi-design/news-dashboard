📰 Top Stories 3
Trade Policy Al Jazeera · BBC · SCMP
US refuses to renew USMCA — $1.8 trillion North American trade pact enters annual review
Washington declined to renew the US-Mexico-Canada Agreement during the mandatory July 1 review. USTR Greer cited trade deficits and Canada's efforts to attract Chinese investment. The pact remains active but now faces annual review periods until its 2036 expiration, creating prolonged uncertainty for ~17 million jobs across North America.
Conflict Al Jazeera · BBC
Amnesty: RSF committed ethnic cleansing and crimes against humanity in Sudan's el-Fasher
Amnesty International documented mass murder, rape, sexual slavery, and forcible transfer by the Rapid Support Forces during the May 2024–October 2025 siege of el-Fasher. The report, based on 246 interviews, follows a UN finding that the assault bore "hallmarks of genocide." Sudan's conflict has killed tens of thousands and displaced nearly 14 million.
Justice BBC · SCMP
Maltese tycoon on trial for ordering murder of journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia
Yorgen Fenech, a prominent businessman, faced trial in Malta nine years after the 2017 car bomb assassination of investigative journalist Daphne Caruana Galizia — dubbed a "one-woman WikiLeaks" for her corruption exposés. Her killing brought down a government and prompted EU rule-of-law scrutiny of Malta, the bloc's smallest member.
