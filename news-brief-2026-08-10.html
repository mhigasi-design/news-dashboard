Netanyahu said Israel rejects the Board of Peace's Gaza document, insisting the IDF "will not carry out any withdrawal until Hamas is genuinely disarmed," and declared no Palestinian state will be established while he is prime minister. Conflict AP · Al Jazeera
Russia, Ukraine report 7 dead, dozens injured in overnight strikes
Russian missiles hit a high-rise apartment block in Kharkiv's Saltivskyi district, killing two, while Ukrainian drones killed five in Russia's Belgorod region, including injuring a 4-year-old boy. Dozens of missiles and drones also struck Odesa's port and grain infrastructure; Zelensky said Moscow was "systematically waging war against global food security."
Climate Al Jazeera · Reuters
Typhoon Dolphin hits eastern China, more than one million evacuated
The most powerful cyclone to hit China this year made landfall near Yuhuan, Zhejiang, with sustained winds of 151km/h. More than 900,000 residents were relocated from Wenzhou alone and Shanghai cancelled roughly 1,500 flights. Dolphin had earlier injured six and cut power to 50,000+ buildings in Japan's Okinawa.
