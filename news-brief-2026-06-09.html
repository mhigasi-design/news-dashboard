Disaster · Asia-Pacific Al Jazeera · SCMP
Philippines 7.8 earthquake kills 35+, triggers tsunami — deadliest quake in years hits Mindanao
A powerful 7.8 magnitude earthquake struck off the coast of Sarangani province in southern Mindanao on Monday, collapsing buildings and schools across General Santos City and triggering a tsunami warning. At least 35 confirmed dead, over 200 injured; a separate landslide in Glan killed 13 more. Tsunami waves struck southern coastal areas with smaller waves recorded in Indonesia, Palau, and southern Japan. President Marcos cancelled classes and mobilised disaster agencies as rescuers searched for missing students in collapsed structures.
Conflict · Middle East SCMP · Al Jazeera
Israel-Iran exchange fire for first time since April truce, then halt — Trump warns Netanyahu: "you'll be on your own"
Israel and Iran traded strikes overnight — the first direct military exchange since an April ceasefire — then both announced a halt. Iran's command said any continued Israeli "aggression" would trigger "much more severe and crushing measures." Privately, Trump told Netanyahu to "be careful, or you will be on your own very soon," according to Axios, as continued Israeli strikes on Beirut jeopardize ongoing US-Iran peace negotiations. Iranian civilians expressed exhaustion and uncertainty about whether the two nations remain at war. 3,637 Lebanese fatalities since March.
Justice · International Al Jazeera · SCMP
ICC prosecutor Karim Khan suspended — member states to vote on removal over sexual misconduct allegations
The International Criminal Court's governing Bureau formally suspended chief prosecutor Karim Khan pending a special session of all 125 member states, where 63 votes would be needed for removal. A UN investigation found "factual basis" for allegations of nonconsensual sexual contact with a female aide, though a three-judge judicial panel deemed findings "not fully conclusive." Khan, who has been on voluntary leave since May 2025, denies wrongdoing and calls the probe retaliation for his pursuit of arrest warrants for Israeli PM Netanyahu, former defence minister Gallant, and Hamas leader Sinwar.
