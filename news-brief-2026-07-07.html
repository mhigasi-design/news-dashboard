NATO · Security NATO.int · NPR
NATO Ankara Summit opens — 32 allies to formalise 5% GDP defence pledge
Trump arrived at the Besstepe compound as the two-day summit opened in Ankara. Core agenda: enforcing the 5% GDP spending target by 2035, accelerating European arms production, and sustaining military aid to Ukraine. €70B in allied defence commitments expected to be formalised. Trump–Zelenskyy meeting planned for Wednesday.
Conflict Kyiv Independent · CBS News
Russia's largest Kyiv strike in months kills 25 — Patriot stocks exhausted
A barrage of 23 ballistic missiles, 39 cruise missiles, 6 hypersonic Zircons and 351 drones struck Kyiv overnight, killing at least 25 and wounding 90+. All ballistic missiles and Zircons hit their targets as Ukraine's interceptor stocks ran out. Zelenskyy will demand emergency Patriot resupply from allies at the Ankara summit.
Middle East Al Jazeera · NBC News
Hamas dissolves Gaza governing body — power transfer to US-backed panel remains in doubt
Hamas announced dissolution of its Government Emergency Committee — the body administering Gaza for nearly two decades — framing it as enabling a US-backed National Committee for Administration of Gaza (NCAG) to take over. Israel dismissed the move as "deception," noting Hamas made no mention of disarmament and its members remain in their posts. The NCAG has been blocked from entering Gaza by Israel for months.
Geopolitics Al Jazeera
Macron visits Syria — first EU leader since Assad's fall, French businesses eye reconstruction
French President Macron held a working dinner in Damascus with Ahmed al-Sharaa, accompanied by CEOs from TotalEnergies and CMA CGM. The visit signals France's intent to anchor a position in Syria's reconstruction. Al-Sharaa travels to Ankara on Tuesday where a meeting with Trump — a significant diplomatic milestone — is expected.
