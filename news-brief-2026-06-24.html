Geopolitics Al Jazeera · SCMP
Iran-US Day 116: Switzerland talks wrap up — $12B in frozen assets, 60-day oil sanctions waiver agreed
Iran's chief negotiator Ghalibaf announced technical talks concluded successfully with a deal releasing $12B in frozen funds for agricultural purchases. The US Treasury waived oil and petrochemical sanctions through August 21. Trump and Tehran continued to dispute the terms of IAEA nuclear inspections access.
Middle East Al Jazeera · SCMP
UN inquiry: Israel deliberately targeted Gaza children as "part of genocide" — 50,000+ killed or wounded
The UN Independent Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory concluded that Israeli forces deliberately killed Palestinian children, constituting evidence of genocide. Commission Chair Muralidhar: "Palestinian children have been deliberately targeted." Israel called the report a "libelous sham." Around 30% of Gaza's dead are children.
Climate Al Jazeera · SCMP
Europe's deadliest heatwave in decades — France hits 43°C (hottest since 1947), 40 drown, Louvre cuts hours
An "Omega block" weather pattern is drawing Saharan air over Europe. France's national temperature average hit 29.8°C; Les Herbiers reached 43°C — highest since 1947. At least 40 people drowned in unsupervised swimming areas. UK temperatures exceeded 39°C. Italy issued red heat alerts for 15 cities. The Eiffel Tower and Louvre reduced operations.
Conflict Al Jazeera · SCMP
Russia cluster missile kills 3 in Kryvyi Rih — rouble at 75, Moscow Exchange down 5%
A Russian cluster munition strike on the central Ukrainian city killed three and wounded 25. Ukraine's drone campaign has hit Russian refineries hard — fuel shortages are spreading across Russian regions. The rouble weakened past 75/USD for the first time since May, and the Moscow Exchange hit its lowest level since March 2023. The Kremlin insists "macroeconomic stability is absolutely ensured."
Middle East Al Jazeera · SCMP
Israel kills 2 near Nabatieh — Hezbollah "finger on trigger"; Washington talks called a "train wreck"
Israeli troops shot and killed two civilians unblocking a road near Nabatieh within Israel's declared security zone. Hezbollah Deputy Chief Qamati said the group is "fully alert" and will retaliate "in kind." A 5th round of US-mediated Israel-Lebanon talks is underway at the State Department, but Israel's ambassador called them a "train wreck" — key disputes: Hezbollah disarmament vs. Israeli withdrawal from southern Lebanon.
