Press Freedom BBC · Al Jazeera
Russia forces closure of Novaya Gazeta, one of its last independent newspapers
Authorities revoked Novaya Gazeta's print license, blocked its website domestically and seized its presses; the paper goes fully dark Nov 9. Founder/editor Sergei Sokolov says "they strangled us" — pressure lands as another senior editor remains under house arrest on separate, critics say politically motivated, charges.
Geopolitics BBC · Al Jazeera
G20 "family photo" cancelled after protests over presence of Russian minister
At the G20 foreign ministers' meeting in Atlanta, the traditional group photo was scrapped after roughly a third of delegations — including Germany, the UK, Canada and Australia — objected to being pictured alongside Russia's Sergey Lavrov, underscoring the bloc's deepening split over Ukraine.
Ukraine War BBC · Al Jazeera
Oleshky ceasefire: hundreds evacuated from Russian-occupied town in Ukraine
A localized truce let an aid convoy reach the Russian-occupied town of Oleshky, evacuating hundreds of residents — some onward to Russia, others via Belarus back into Ukraine. It's the first joint humanitarian operation of its kind since 2022, even as fighting continues on other parts of the front.
Diplomacy Guardian · Al Jazeera
Canada and EU announce new alliance open to like-minded countries
At a Montreal summit, PM Mark Carney and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen unveiled the "Alliance for the Future," open to other nations, covering AI/supercomputing access, critical-minerals investment and support toward a €90bn Ukraine loan. Trump has called a Canada "associate member" arrangement a possible "hostile act."
