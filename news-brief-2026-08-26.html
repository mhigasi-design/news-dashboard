Geopolitics BBC · Guardian
CIA director John Ratcliffe makes unannounced Moscow visit
Flight-tracking data revealed a US military aircraft flying to Moscow via Latvia, carrying CIA Director John Ratcliffe on his first known visit to Russia as CIA chief. The FT reported the Trump administration had asked Kyiv to pause long-range strikes on major Russian cities during the trip. Neither the CIA nor the White House has commented.

Ukraine BBC · Guardian
UK PM Burnham vows support for Ukraine despite "outrageous threats" from Russia
On his first foreign trip as PM, Andy Burnham chaired the "coalition of the willing" in Kyiv and confirmed defence firm MBDA will hand Ukraine blueprints for British components in the Storm Shadow missile, enabling domestic production. The Kremlin accused Burnham of wanting the conflict to continue; he said allies would "keep up the pressure on Russia."

Climate BBC · Guardian
Tornado tears through French village of Pomas, dozens hurt
A tornado with winds up to 117km/h struck Pomas, near Carcassonne in south-west France, injuring 39 people — 15 hospitalised, two critically — and damaging around 300 homes. A stage of the Vuelta a España was cancelled due to the same storm system; part of a centuries-old castle collapsed.

Climate AP · Al Jazeera
Indonesians hold mass prayers for rain as wildfire haze worsens
Authorities in South Sumatra held a mass Islamic rain prayer as forest and peatland fires across at least 10 provinces generated choking haze, with South Sumatra recording the most hotspots (1,429) nationwide. Nearly 940 sq km burned in July alone; smoke has drifted into Malaysia and Brunei.
