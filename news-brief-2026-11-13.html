Corporate BBC · Al Jazeera
Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO after affair allegations, Colette Kress named interim chief
Nvidia's board acted after reports Huang had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy and a second affair with a direct report. The resignation triggered an FBI/Congressional-interest review and knocked Nvidia's market value below $4 trillion.
Geopolitics BBC · Al Jazeera (AP)
US envoys meet Zelenskyy in Dublin after talks with Putin in Moscow
Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner spent two-plus hours with Putin in the Kremlin before flying to brief Zelenskyy on the sidelines of the European Political Community summit. Witkoff says Putin has agreed to let European negotiators join the next round of talks; a prisoner exchange was also agreed.
Middle East BBC · Al Jazeera
Israeli strike on Gaza hospital kills six Palestinians, health ministry says
A missile hit the surgical wards and intensive care unit at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis, the largest hospital in southern Gaza, killing surgeon Dr Rizk Abu Shaira and five others and wounding about 20. It's the first strike on the hospital since the October 2025 ceasefire took hold.
Technology BBC · Al Jazeera (Reuters)
Microsoft outage sees Teams, Outlook and Copilot suffer "connectivity issues"
Microsoft said its infrastructure was hit by "elevated load," disrupting Outlook email, Teams and Copilot for tens of thousands of users worldwide on Thursday. Services were being progressively restored.
