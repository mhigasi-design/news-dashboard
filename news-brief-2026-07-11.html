Disaster Al Jazeera · SCMP
Spain wildfire kills 12 in Almeria — deadliest in regional history
A fast-moving blaze near Los Gallardos and Bedar swept through Andalusia Thursday, claiming at least 12 lives with 19 still unaccounted for. Extreme heat pushing 40°C and strong winds drove the flames; over 300 emergency personnel mobilised. Many victims are believed to be foreign tourists, including British nationals found in their vehicle.
Conflict Al Jazeera · Reuters
Russia kills 5 in Kramatorsk and Zaporizhzhia — Ukraine drones hit Russian refineries
Russian aerial bombardment struck two eastern Ukrainian cities on Friday, killing five civilians. Ukraine's military responded by attacking two major refineries, an oil terminal, and ~50 fuel vessels in the Sea of Azov. Russian officials acknowledged fuel shortages now affecting roughly a third of the country's population.
Climate SCMP · Reuters
Typhoon Bavi makes landfall in Fujian — cloud cover expands to 1.4M km²
Once a super typhoon, Bavi weakened but expanded dramatically — its eye vanished while cloud coverage grew to 1.4 million square kilometres, 1,263 times Hong Kong's size. Expected to hit between Fuqing (Fujian) and Wenling (Zhejiang), disrupting flights at 10+ airports. Experts warn energy dispersal across the broader system could make it more hazardous despite lower wind speeds.
Policy Al Jazeera · AP
EU finds Meta's Instagram and Facebook breach DSA — fines up to 6% of turnover
The European Commission concluded a two-year investigation finding Meta's autoplay, infinite scrolling, and personalised algorithms push users into "autopilot mode" in violation of the Digital Services Act. Meta faces fines up to 6% of annual global turnover and must disable addictive defaults. The company disputed the findings and has months to respond before a final ruling.
Corporate SCMP · Bloomberg
Apple sues OpenAI for trade secret theft — names ex-chief hardware officer
Apple filed a 41-page federal complaint in San Jose alleging OpenAI orchestrated a campaign to steal hardware trade secrets by poaching employees, including Tang Yew Tan (now OpenAI's chief hardware officer). The suit marks a dramatic reversal of the companies' 2024 ChatGPT integration partnership and could complicate OpenAI's anticipated IPO.
US Policy Al Jazeera · AP · Reuters
Trump fires all remaining Election Assistance Commission members ahead of midterms
Trump dismissed both Democratic EAC commissioners by email; the lone Republican resigned in protest, leaving the Congressional watchdog fully vacant months before the November midterms. The EAC, created in 2002, is required by law to maintain equal partisan representation. The move follows a Supreme Court ruling expanding presidential power to remove independent agency heads without cause.
