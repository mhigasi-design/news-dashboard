📰 Top Stories 5
Disaster Al Jazeera · SCMP
Venezuela twin earthquakes (7.2 & 7.5) kill 188+, injure 1,500 — state of emergency declared
Strongest quakes in Venezuela in over a century struck northern coast Wednesday evening. La Guaira is worst-hit; Caracas' Simon Bolivar airport remains closed. Over 200 still trapped. Preliminary economic damage estimated at 1–7% of GDP. Eleven nations — including several with severed diplomatic ties — offered aid within hours.
Conflict Al Jazeera · SCMP
Cargo ship Ever Lovely hit by drone in Hormuz — IMO suspends 11,000-mariner evacuation
The Singapore-flagged container ship was struck 14km off Oman by a suspected drone Thursday. The IMO immediately paused its evacuation initiative — designed to shepherd ~600 stranded vessels through two Hormuz corridors. Daily transits remain at ~70 vs. 120 pre-war. Oil prices edging higher on renewed supply fears.
Geopolitics Al Jazeera · SCMP
US Senate 50–48 passes Iran War Powers resolution — first dual-chamber War Powers Act
Four Republicans (Cassidy, Murkowski, Collins, Paul) crossed party lines to direct Trump to withdraw forces absent explicit congressional authorization. Trump dismissed it: "There are no limits" to executive power. Iran nuclear talks remain deadlocked over inspection access and $24B in frozen assets.
Policy SCMP
EU releases €3B first tranche of €90B Ukraine recovery fund at Gdansk conference
Ukrainian PM Svyrydenko confirmed the disbursement at the Ukraine Recovery Conference in Gdansk. A further €6B for drone production follows "in coming days." The EU has provided €200B in support since 2022. The announcement coincides with Ukraine's formal EU membership negotiations, opened June 15.
Geopolitics Al Jazeera
Kenya: 355 arrested on 2nd anniversary of 2024 anti-tax crackdown that killed 60
Police used tear gas and water cannons to disperse mourners placing flowers at parliament, two years after security forces killed 60 when demonstrators stormed the building opposing a now-scrapped finance bill. President Ruto has allocated $15.5M for victims; activists say accountability demands remain unmet.
