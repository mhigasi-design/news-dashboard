Conflict Al Jazeera · Multiple
Israel seizes Beaufort Castle, orders 1M+ evacuated — troops push north of Litani
Israeli forces crossed the Litani River — a first since 2006 — and seized the 12th-century Beaufort Castle near Nabatieh. Netanyahu ordered troops to "expand the ground manoeuvre." New displacement orders require all residents south of the Zahrani River to evacuate, rendering one-fifth of Lebanon inaccessible. Total killed: 3,412; wounded: 10,269. Qatar, France, and Jordan condemned the offensive.
Elections Bloomberg · CNN
Colombia: De la Espriella 43.77% vs Cepeda 41.08% — June 21 runoff set in upset result
Independent businessman Abelardo de la Espriella outperformed every pre-election poll to lead leftist senator Iván Cepeda into a June 21 runoff. Polls had Cepeda at ~33% — results flip the pre-election narrative. Right-wing Uribista Paloma Valencia placed a distant third at 6.9%. With no candidate clearing 50%, the right-centre vs left showdown heads to a runoff.
Global Health Al Jazeera · WHO
Ebola confirmed cases nearly double in 2 days — WHO chief in Bunia; no vaccine for this strain
DRC confirmed cases surged from 121 to 225 in two days; total suspected cases: 1,028 across DRC and Uganda. WHO DG Tedros flew to Bunia, Ituri province, calling for community-led response. MSF: "one of the fastest-spreading Ebola outbreaks ever recorded." The Bundibugyo strain has no approved vaccine or treatment. EU deployed supplies; US pledged $112M. Armed group attacks on health workers continue to hamper containment.
