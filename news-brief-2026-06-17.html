📰 Top Stories 5
Middle East Al Jazeera · AFP/SCMP
Iran demands Lebanon withdrawal as peace MOU condition — Israeli strikes kill 4 in Nabatieh
FM Araghchi calls Lebanon "most important issue" in the June 15 MOU: "ending the war in Lebanon is an inseparable part of the complete end of the war." Netanyahu flatly refuses to leave the 570 km² southern Lebanon buffer zone. Iran warns Israel of "a hard response." 3,826 killed in Lebanon since March; Hormuz reopening awaited by global shippers.
Global Health Al Jazeera · ECDC
Africa CDC: DRC Ebola could surpass 2014 West Africa outbreak — 837 cases, 26,000 contacts untraceable
DG Jean Kaseya: "If we don't stop the outbreak very soon, it will be worse than what we had in West Africa" — referring to the 11,000-death 2014 outbreak. 808 confirmed in DRC (738 in Ituri), 19 in Uganda. Red Cross warns it could last a year. No licensed vaccine or treatment for the Bundibugyo strain; funding secured is less than one-fifth of $518M target.
Policy Reuters · SCMP
EU Parliament votes to slash US import duties, meeting Trump's July 4 tariff deadline after 11-month delay
European Parliament approved removing duties on US industrial goods under the July 2025 Turnberry framework deal in exchange for 15% Trump tariffs on EU exports. Von der Leyen: "A deal is a deal — the EU is delivering its part." Trump had threatened "much higher" levies if the EU failed to act by July 4. Final approval from EU member states expected June 26.
Conflict Al Jazeera · UK MoD
UK Royal Marines seize Russian shadow-fleet tanker Smyrtos in Channel — first-ever boarding; six vessels divert
Pre-dawn helicopter raid off Dorset detained Smyrtos (Cameroon-flagged, Hong Kong-owned) carrying 700,000 barrels of Russian oil. Six other tankers immediately changed course away from the English Channel, adding ~926km to journeys. The operation follows similar actions by France, the US, and Nordic nations targeting Russia's sanctions-evasion networks.
Geopolitics SCMP · Reuters
G7 in Evian-les-Bains: Trump meets Zelensky, urges Putin to "make a deal" on Ukraine
Trump called the Trump-Zelensky bilateral "very good" and described war casualties on both sides as "ridiculous." Zelensky and European allies aimed to demonstrate Ukraine's strengthened battlefield position to bolster Kyiv's leverage ahead of any negotiations. Putin has not responded to direct-talks proposals. Russia strikes continued across Ukraine in parallel.
🌡 Market Sentiment
😐
Post-Deal Recalibration
Iran-US MOU signed June 15 has pushed oil back to $75.86 — a significant drop from the $87-109 wartime range, easing the inflation pressure that had been building for months. However, equity markets are drifting lower: S&P -0.57%, Nasdaq -1.15%, Hang Seng -1.40%. Iran's new Lebanon conditions and shipowners holding off on Hormuz re-entry are keeping geopolitical risk on the table. US 10Y yields ticking down to 4.43% suggests mild safe-haven demand.
