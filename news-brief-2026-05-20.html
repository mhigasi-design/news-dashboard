Gaza Al Jazeera · WaPo · PBS
Israel commandos seize entire Gaza aid flotilla — 430 activists detained, US sanctions organizers
All 50+ vessels of the Global Sumud Flotilla intercepted ~268km from Gaza in broad daylight; activists boarded in life vests with hands raised. Turkey's FM called it "piracy." The US simultaneously imposed sanctions on four flotilla organizers — a coordinated signal to deter future attempts.
Geopolitics Pravda EN · Bloomberg
Putin & Xi sign "multipolar world" declaration — 40 deals, nuclear & AI cooperation
At the conclusion of Putin's 2-day Beijing state visit, Russia and China adopted a joint declaration rejecting the "unipolar world order" and signed ~40 bilateral agreements covering nuclear energy, AI, trade, construction, and media. The 47-page strategic partnership document is the most expansive since the 2022 "no-limits" partnership.
Middle East Al Jazeera · NPR · PBS
Drone strikes UAE's Barakah nuclear plant — IAEA expresses "grave concern," no radiation leak
A drone sparked a fire at an electrical generator on the perimeter of the UAE's $20B Barakah nuclear plant in Abu Dhabi. Air defences downed two drones; a third got through. No injuries or radiological release. UAE says drones came from the "western border"; no group has claimed responsibility. IAEA chief Grossi warned attacks on nuclear facilities are "unacceptable."
Markets CNN Business · CNBC
30-year US Treasury hits 5.2% — highest since 2007 as bond vigilantes return
The 30-yr yield briefly touched 5.197%, extending a bond market selloff driven by Iran-linked energy inflation, rising US debt concerns, and no Fed cut in sight. Equities fell for a third straight session — S&P 500 -0.67%, Nasdaq -0.84%. A BofA survey found 62% of global fund managers now expect 30-yr yields to hit 6%, a level not seen since late 1999.
Economy NPR · CNBC · CNN Business
Kevin Warsh confirmed as Fed Chair — no 2026 rate cuts, first cut not until mid-2027
Senate confirmed Warsh 54-45 on May 13 — the closest confirmation in the modern era. He officially replaced Jerome Powell on May 15. BofA now expects the Fed to hold through 2026, with the first cut coming in July 2027. Warsh has signaled he sees persistent inflation as his primary risk.
Global Health CDC · WHO · Al Jazeera
Ebola escalates — 536 suspected cases, 134 deaths; CDC evacuates American from DRC
The DRC/Uganda Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak — declared a WHO PHEIC last week — is accelerating. As of May 19: 536 suspected cases, 105 probable, 34 confirmed, 134 deaths across 9 health zones in Ituri Province. There is no licensed vaccine or specific therapeutic. The CDC is evacuating at least one American who tested positive, and has activated enhanced screening at US entry points.
