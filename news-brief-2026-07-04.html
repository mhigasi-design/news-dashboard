Geopolitics Al Jazeera · CNN · SCMP
Iran begins week-long state funeral for Khamenei — 20 million expected in Tehran
Seven days of ceremonies began July 3 for Supreme Leader Khamenei, killed in a US-Israeli strike on February 28. More than 50 delegations have paid respects — European nations excluded. Iran's government says national unity has grown stronger, not weaker, since the assassination. Burial at the Imam Reza shrine in Mashhad on July 9.
Middle East Al Jazeera · Bloomberg
Iran threatens "forceful response" on Hormuz — China urges free passage, Europe braces for fees
Iran's Khatam al-Anbiya command warned ships on unapproved Hormuz routes face "an immediate and forceful response," citing a recent vessel grounding. China called for unhindered passage; Bloomberg reports European governments are privately accepting some transit fees as inevitable. At least 49 attacks on commercial vessels recorded since February 28.
Middle East Al Jazeera · CNN
Israel bombs 10+ Lebanon sites on day 1 of Khamenei funeral — framework agreement fraying
The Israeli army struck more than ten locations it says Hezbollah was using in Lebanon, even as Khamenei's funeral opened in Tehran. Total killed in Lebanon and Israel since March 2 has reached 4,301. A US-brokered framework agreement signed last month did not include Hezbollah as a party, and analysts warn it has no durable mechanism for enforcement.
Ukraine Al Jazeera · Bloomberg
Russia's June offensive collapses — 40,000 troops killed; drone campaign throttles fuel supply
Russia's military advance, which had appeared to be making ground in May, stalled and reversed through June as Ukraine's asymmetric drone campaign degraded an estimated 40% of Russian oil refining capacity. Moscow is scrambling to reroute fuel supplies and maintain price caps. NATO summit in Ankara is set for July 7, where Zelenskyy is expected to push for advanced air defense systems.
