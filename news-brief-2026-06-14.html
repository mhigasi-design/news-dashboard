Middle East Fox News · CNBC · CBS News
Trump: Iran-US deal to be signed today — Hormuz "open to all" the moment ink dries
On Truth Social, Trump announced "the Deal is scheduled to get signed tomorrow [June 14]" and that "immediately after it is signed, the Hormuz Strait is OPEN TO ALL." Iran's FM said the exact date is uncertain but a signing "in the coming days cannot be ruled out." The MOU covers a 60-day ceasefire, Hormuz reopening, and Iran committing to no nuclear weapons for 15–20 years. Israeli officials warned the terms "endanger Israel's security interests." US blockade has redirected 141 commercial vessels since April.
Middle East Al Jazeera · Washington Post
Iran sets Khamenei funeral for July 4–9 — burial in Mashhad, 3.5 months after assassination
Iran's government announced that Ali Khamenei, killed in US-Israeli airstrikes on February 28, will have state funeral ceremonies in Tehran (July 4–6) and Qom (July 7), with burial at the shrine of Imam Reza in Mashhad on July 9. The delay was attributed to ongoing war conditions. Khamenei's son Mojtaba, now Supreme Leader, has not appeared in public since assuming power. Security concerns are cited as a major factor in the unprecedented 4.5-month gap between death and state burial.
Asia-Pacific Reuters · Arab News · US News
North Korea declares denuclearization "terminated irreversibly" — condemns US-South Korea nuclear talks
North Korea's Foreign Ministry issued a statement Sunday declaring its status as a nuclear-armed state is permanent and that "the 'denuclearisation' is an irreversibly finalised matter." The statement came directly in response to the US-South Korea Nuclear Consultative Group (NCG) talks in Seoul, where both sides strengthened nuclear deterrence postures. Pyongyang warned that "meaningless rhetoric against the DPRK and cooperation in posing a nuclear threat can never affect" its position. The declaration hardens North Korea's stance as Iran edges toward a nuclear-free deal with Washington.
Europe Al Jazeera · Euronews · Washington Post
EU opens accession negotiations with Ukraine and Moldova — formal talks begin June 15 in Luxembourg
All 27 EU member states agreed Friday to formally open accession negotiations with Ukraine and Moldova, with the first negotiating cluster — covering rule of law, human rights, and judiciary — set to open Monday in Luxembourg. Hungary's new PM Péter Magyar lifted Budapest's 18-month veto after securing minority rights commitments from Kyiv. Both countries see EU membership as a security guarantee against Russian aggression. Ukraine has fulfilled the final pre-conditions and aims to close multiple chapters within 2026.
Global Health ECDC · WHO · CDC
Ebola Bundibugyo: 695 confirmed cases, 138 deaths across DRC and Uganda — 41 new cases in 24 hours
As of June 12, DRC reported 676 confirmed cases and 136 deaths, with 262 in isolation. Uganda has 19 confirmed cases and 2 deaths, with no new cases since June 5. The DRC case count jumped by 41 in a single day (June 11–12), with Ituri province accounting for 629 of the DRC total. The WHO-Africa CDC $518M joint response plan is now operational. No licensed vaccine exists for the Bundibugyo strain. The next WHO situation report is due June 15.
