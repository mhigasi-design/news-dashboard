Army Secretary Dan Driscoll resigns after clashes with Hegseth
Driscoll, the youngest-ever Army secretary and a close Vance ally, submitted his resignation Aug 31 after 18 months, citing Hegseth blocking Army transformation efforts and firing generals. The White House confirmed the departure Sept 1.
Disaster Al Jazeera · CNN · Guardian
Nepal-Tibet flood death toll tops 1,000
Confirmed deaths reached 1,007 in Nepal plus 16 in Tibet as of Sept 1, with nearly 4,600 still missing. Rescuers are racing to reach roughly 900 workers trapped in flooded hydropower tunnels.
Conflict AP · Al Jazeera · Guardian
Russia pounds Kyiv for sixth straight night, killing 12
A 12+ hour barrage of missiles and 218 drones hit Kyiv and Odesa regions Sept 1, killing 12 — including 6 railway workers at a depot — and wounding over a dozen. Ukraine's foreign minister renewed calls for more Patriot interceptors.
Geopolitics BBC · Guardian
Germany blames Russia for drone attack at Leipzig airport
German authorities said Sept 1 that Russia was behind a drone incursion at Leipzig/Halle airport, escalating tensions between Berlin and Moscow over a string of recent European airspace incidents.
Corporate AP · Bloomberg
Apple has a new CEO for the first time in 15 years
John Ternus, formerly SVP of Hardware Engineering, took over as CEO effective Sept 1, ending Tim Cook's 15-year run; Cook becomes executive chairman. Ternus's first big test is the Sept 9 iPhone 18 launch amid pressure to catch up on AI.
