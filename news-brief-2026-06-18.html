Russian frigate fires warning shots at British civilian yacht in English Channel
The Russian frigate Admiral Grigorovich fired warning shots at the UK-flagged yacht Bright Future approximately 20 nautical miles south of the Isle of Wight. PM Starmer called it "reckless" at the G7; Russia claimed collision risk. HMS Mersey was shadowing the vessel at the time.
Policy CNN · Bloomberg
Fed holds rates but hawkish dot plot signals 2026 hike — markets sell off sharply
Nine of nineteen Fed officials signalled a rate hike before year-end, surprising markets. Two-year Treasury yields jumped 16bp to 4.21%. Chair Warsh cited tariff pass-through and post-Iran-deal oil price residuals as the main inflation risks demanding vigilance.
Corporate Al Jazeera · SCMP
US judge rules Meng Wanzhou's admissions admissible — Huawei September trial strengthened
A federal judge in Brooklyn ruled that Meng's 2021 DPA admissions — acknowledging Huawei misled a bank about Iran sanctions compliance — can be used as evidence against Huawei itself. The ruling rejected Huawei's right-to-silence argument and significantly fortifies the US government's case.
US Politics Al Jazeera · Bloomberg · CNN
Georgia GOP runoffs: Trump wins Senate pick, loses governor race to $100M self-funder
Congressman Mike Collins, Trump's Senate pick, defeats Derek Dooley for the GOP nomination ahead of a November race vs. Jon Ossoff. But in the governor contest, Lt. Governor Burt Jones lost to healthcare billionaire Rick Jackson, who spent ~$100M of his own money — a rare Trump endorsement defeat.
