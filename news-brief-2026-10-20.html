Curfew imposed in parts of central Paris and other cities after rioting across France
Overnight curfews now cover Place de la République and Les Halles in Paris, plus parts of Marseille, Bordeaux, Nice, Lyon and Lille, after a weekend of riots and looting. Houthi missile strike on Yemen camp kills 50 security recruits, authorities say
Three ballistic missiles hit the al-Alam training camp near Ataq in Yemen's Shabwa province, killing 50+ recruits to the internationally recognised government's security forces and injuring around 200. Yemen's Presidential Leadership Council called it a "criminal attack" and promised a "firm response"; the Houthis said they targeted "Saudi enemy concentrations."
Published ~00:20 HKT, 20 Oct 2026
North America · Policy BBC · SCMP
Albertans cast ballots on potential separation from Canada
Alberta — home to the world's fourth-largest oil reserves — is voting on whether to stay in Canada or trigger a binding independence referendum. More than 1.35 million people voted early (43%+ of the electorate), a record turnout signal. A "Yes" majority would kick-start the formal separation process; Indigenous Albertans have been working for months to block the vote, arguing it breaches land treaties.
Live, as of 06:57 HKT, 20 Oct 2026
Corporate BBC · SCMP
Musk and Anthropic pledge public share giveaways
Elon Musk said he will give one share of SpaceX (which owns xAI) to every child in America next year. The announcement came hours after Anthropic pledged 5% of its shares to a fund for "public good" donations ahead of a potential IPO that could value the AI firm above $1tn. Both moves land as Big Tech faces mounting pressure over AI's societal impact; SpaceX itself set the record for the largest-ever IPO in June, raising $85.7bn.
