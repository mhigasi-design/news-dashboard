UK Politics Al Jazeera · CNN
Andy Burnham takes office as UK's 7th prime minister in 10 years
The "King of the North" and former Greater Manchester mayor was formally appointed by King Charles III, succeeding Keir Starmer who resigned in June. Burnham pledged a "new political and economic model" and vowed to govern for all regions, not just London. Trump promptly called the UK a "poverty stricken disaster."
Middle East Al Jazeera
Hamas elects Khalil al-Hayya as new overall leader after run-off with Meshaal
Al-Hayya, a Gaza-born veteran of Hamas since 1987, won an internal run-off against former leader Khaled Meshaal. The 65-year-old was previously central to ceasefire negotiations and is described as closer to Iran than Arab governments. Four of his children have been killed in Israeli strikes.
Iran War Al Jazeera · CBS News
US strikes Iran for 9th straight night — service member killed in Iraq, ship on fire near Hormuz
A US soldier died during controlled detonation of an Iranian drone in northern Iraq, bringing total American deaths since February 28 to 17. CENTCOM continued overnight strikes on Iranian targets. A vessel caught fire near Oman after being struck by an unknown projectile, keeping Hormuz transit under pressure.
Corporate CNN · SCMP
Judge freezes $110B Paramount-Warner merger for 14 days — antitrust ruling
Federal Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín issued a temporary restraining order blocking the close of Paramount Skydance's $110B takeover of Warner Bros. Discovery, ruling the deal "likely" violates antitrust law. The companies had aimed to close as soon as July 22. A full hearing is set for August 3.
