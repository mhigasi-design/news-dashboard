📰 Top Stories 4
Europe / NATO Al Jazeera · BBC · Guardian · SCMP
NATO jets scramble as Russian missile detonates in Poland
A Russian Kh-101 cruise missile entered Polish airspace and detonated near the village of Tarnawa-Kolonia, prompting NATO fighter jets to scramble. It happened during overnight Russian strikes on Ukraine that killed at least eight civilians — the latest in a pattern of airspace violations on NATO's eastern flank. Poland's PM says the missile was "probably Russian."
Middle East Al Jazeera · DW
US launches "heavy wave" of new attacks on Iran
Washington resumed strikes on Iranian military targets after a five-day pause, hitting Qeshm island among other sites. Iranian media report a couple and their two-year-old child were killed. Despite the escalation, Oman-mediated talks over the Strait of Hormuz are continuing.
Migration Al Jazeera · BBC · France24 · DW · SCMP
Spain's Ceuta declares emergency as migrant crossings surge
Between 1,500 and 2,000 migrants have reached Ceuta by sea from Morocco over the past 10 days, with hundreds more arriving today. A Guardia Civil spokesperson called it "a slow trickle since the Supreme Court's ruling" that "today has been an explosion." Spain is deploying additional troops.
Corporate Al Jazeera · BBC · Guardian · DW
UEFA members vote to boycott FIFA over World Cup investor plan
All 55 UEFA members unanimously agreed to boycott FIFA competitions if president Gianni Infantino proceeds with a $20bn plan to sell 20% of a new World Cup commercial subsidiary to private investors. UEFA's message: "The World Cup is not for sale."
