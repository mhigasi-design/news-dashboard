Geopolitics AP · SCMP
Zelensky makes first-ever visit to Russia-friendly Serbia
Zelensky arrived in Belgrade Friday for his first presidential visit to Serbia, one of the few European countries maintaining friendly relations with Russia. He'll meet President Vucic and PM Djuro Macut to discuss economic ties, EU relations and security; a senior Ukrainian official called it an attempt to "pull the Serbs away from Russia's side." Vucic says Serbia won't change its position on sanctioning Moscow.
US Policy Guardian · Al Jazeera
US appeals court blocks Trump's $400m White House ballroom
The DC Circuit upheld a preliminary injunction in a 2-1 ruling, halting the 90,000 sq ft ballroom project — built after Trump demolished the East Wing — without congressional authorization. Judges wrote "each President is a temporary tenant, not the owner" of the White House. The ruling is stayed two weeks for a Supreme Court appeal; Trump called it "totally wrong."
Economy Guardian · SCMP
Trump renews effort to fire Fed Governor Lisa Cook
The White House sent Cook a letter saying Trump is "considering" removing her, alleging mortgage-fraud-related conduct potentially punishable by up to 30 years in prison, and gave her three weeks to respond. Her lawyer calls the allegations "baseless." It's the second time this week Trump has revived an action the Supreme Court blocked earlier this year, after birthright citizenship.
Middle East SCMP · Al Jazeera
Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan sign mutual-defence pact
The three Sunni-majority US allies signed the "Mecca Joint Defence Agreement," declaring an attack on one an attack on all, as Iran and its allies continue firing on Saudi Arabia and Gulf states and blockading energy shipments since the Feb 28 US-Israel strikes on Iran. Specific military commitments weren't detailed.
