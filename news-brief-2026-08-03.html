Middle East BBC · AP
Trump cancels Iran strikes subject to deal being made "rapidly"
Trump says the "perimeters" of a deal have been agreed after Iran and regional allies asked Washington to hold off on strikes. Al Jazeera reports negotiations resume Monday, though no breakthrough has been reached on reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Oil fell over 4% on the de-escalation.
Climate AP · NPR
Eastern Washington fires burn 600 structures, force 60,000 evacuations
A fast-moving wildfire complex — the Old Trails, Autumn Lane and Fairview fires — swept through Spokane County, destroying over 600 structures. Washington's governor declared a statewide emergency and activated the National Guard.
Climate BBC · Guardian
Two crew killed as firefighting helicopters collide in Greece
A Danish and a Greek national died when two firefighting helicopters collided while battling wildfires near Athens; a British pilot and another Greek crew member survived. Greece is fighting a renewed wildfire outbreak fanned by gale-force winds.
Conflict BBC · Guardian
Moscow restaurant bombing kills three, injures 21
A bomb carried into an Italian restaurant in Moscow by a woman killed at least three people and injured 21, Russian media report. Authorities have not yet named a suspect or confirmed a motive.
Culture BBC · Al Jazeera
Massive Attack permanently barred from Singapore over Palestine flag
Singapore has permanently barred Massive Attack's two founding members, Robert Del Naja and Grant Marshall, after they unfurled a Palestinian flag during a Star Theatre concert on 29 July, citing rules against displaying foreign political emblems.
