Ukraine · War Al Jazeera · CNN
Russia's largest-ever ballistic missile attack on Kyiv — 41 missiles, 125 drones; Patriot stocks critically low
At least 1 killed and 16 wounded in Kyiv; additional deaths in Kharkiv, Zaporizhia and Odesa. Ukraine intercepted ~half the ballistic missiles and 108 of 125 drones, but Acting FM Sybiha warned Patriot interceptor stocks are now dangerously low. Strikes follow Zelensky's recent dismissal of Defence Minister Fedorov.
Middle East · Conflict Al Jazeera · CNN
US-Iran: 8th consecutive night of strikes — 2 soldiers killed in Jordan; Iran hits Kuwait air base
US targeted Sirik, Qeshm Island and Shadegan in Khuzestan, destroying a civilian desalination plant. Iran retaliated by hitting Al-Adiri ammunition depot and air defense systems at Ali Al Salem base in Kuwait. Jordan intercepted 3 Iranian missiles. 16 US personnel killed and 430+ wounded since the conflict began in late February.
World Cup 2026 Al Jazeera · CNN
Spain wins FIFA World Cup 2026 — Torres scores in 106th minute to beat 10-man Argentina 1-0 (AET)
Ferran Torres tapped in off Nico Williams' header at MetLife Stadium. Enzo Fernandez received a second yellow card just before the end of normal time, leaving Argentina with 10 men. Messi, 39, finished with 8 goals and 4 assists but couldn't lift his side — likely his final World Cup appearance. Spain become only the second nation to win consecutive Euro and World Cup titles.
Natural Disaster Al Jazeera · Bloomberg
Twin earthquakes strike Peru's Junin region — 5 killed, 21 injured, two hospitals damaged
A magnitude 5.1 quake struck the mountainous Chupaca province at 9:20 pm local time, followed by a 3.7 aftershock. Approximately 48 homes were destroyed and 300 people displaced. Two hospitals in the regional capital Huancayo were damaged. Peru sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire, which accounts for ~85% of global seismic activity.
