Disaster BBC · Al Jazeera
Rescuers scramble for survivors with 180 dead in Colombia earthquake
Colombia has seen a number of aftershocks since the quake struck, with rescue teams still freeing survivors trapped in collapsed buildings. Al Jazeera reports roughly 2,700 people remain reported missing as the search continues.
Middle East BBC · Al Jazeera
US says it fired missiles to disable ship breaking Iran blockade
US Central Command says a helicopter hit the engine room of a Panama-flagged cargo vessel in the Gulf of Oman that it accused of trying to run the blockade around Iran. Tensions over the Strait of Hormuz remain high.
Policy BBC · Al Jazeera · SCMP
Lebanon parliament votes to abolish death penalty
The new law, which now goes to the cabinet for approval, would make Lebanon the first country in the Middle East to end capital punishment. A majority of the 128-member parliament backed the bill; Hezbollah's bloc was the exception.
Conflict BBC · Al Jazeera
Russia using North Korean missiles to strike Ukraine, Zelensky says
Ukraine's president says strikes on Zaporizhzhia involved ballistic missiles supplied by North Korea. BBC reports seven killed in the strikes; Al Jazeera's tally across the wider barrage puts the toll higher.
