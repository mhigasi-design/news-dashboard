Middle East Al Jazeera · CNN
Ben-Gvir posts video of kneeling flotilla detainees — 15+ nations summon Israeli envoys
Israel's National Security Minister filmed himself taunting 430 bound activists at Ashdod port, saying "Welcome to Israel. We are the landowners here." Italy, France, Canada, Germany and 11 others summoned ambassadors. Netanyahu called it "inconsistent with Israel's values." Israel began deporting activists Friday. The video dismantled Israel's $700M state Hasbara budget in an instant.
Russia · Europe Bloomberg · Al Jazeera
Putin & Lukashenko personally oversee joint nuclear drills — Yars ICBM & Zircon launched
First time both presidents have directly participated in a nuclear exercise. Russia's Defence Ministry confirmed test launches of a Yars intercontinental ballistic missile, a Zircon hypersonic missile, and an Iskander-M in Belarus. Ukraine placed its northern border regions on maximum alert. NATO called the exercises "a deliberate escalation signal."
US-China · Taiwan SCMP · CNN · Al Jazeera
Trump signals direct call with Taiwan's Lai — Beijing warns of "turbulence," unprecedented since 1979
Trump told reporters at Joint Base Andrews he would speak with Taiwan's President Lai Ching-te, the first direct US-Taiwan presidential contact since 1979. Beijing's foreign ministry said China's opposition to "official US exchanges with China's Taiwan region" is "consistent, clear and firm." Xi warned Trump just last week that Taiwan missteps could cause "conflict." Taiwan's Lai said he'd be "happy to talk."
Iran · Middle East Al Jazeera · CNBC
Iran reviews US war-ending proposal as Pakistan army chief flies to Tehran
Iran is reviewing Washington's latest proposal as Trump's self-imposed 2–3 day ultimatum expires. Pakistan's military chief Gen. Asim Munir departed for Tehran to push Iran toward a "middle ground." Key sticking points remain: Iran insists nuclear program is off the table in phase one; Washington wants a 12-year enrichment freeze. Oil slipped to ~$108 on deal optimism after peaking at $112.
Tech · IPO CNBC · Bloomberg · CNN
SpaceX retail access confirmed via Robinhood, Fidelity & Schwab — roadshow June 8
SpaceX confirmed a portion of SPCX shares will be sold directly through Robinhood, Fidelity and Charles Schwab — opening its $75B raise to individual investors for the first time. The IPO targets a $1.75T valuation, which would make Elon Musk the first trillionaire. Musk retains 85.1% of voting power via Class B super-voting shares. Roadshow begins June 8; target listing June 11.
Global Health Al Jazeera · CNN · Bloomberg
Ebola vaccine gap: no approved treatment for Bundibugyo strain as outbreak hits 600 cases
Scientists warn there is no approved vaccine or treatment for the rare Bundibugyo strain driving the DRC–Uganda outbreak. WHO reported 600 suspected cases and 139 deaths Friday. Cases have spread to urban centres including Kampala, Goma and Bunia. The strain carries up to 50% lethality. The US is enforcing a 30-day entry restriction on travellers from DRC, Uganda and South Sudan.
