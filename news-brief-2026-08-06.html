Security Al Jazeera
Germany investigates explosive device attached to drone at Leipzig airport
A drone rigged with a detonator was found overnight beside a Ukrainian Antonov An-124 cargo plane at Leipzig/Halle airport; a second object collided with a freighter diverted to Hanover. Federal police say the overflights could have been organised by Russian agents.
War Al Jazeera
Russian attacks kill 17, exploiting Ukraine's lack of missile interceptors
Zelenskyy says Russia fired 24 ballistic missiles, four cruise missiles and 115 drones at Kyiv and its region overnight; none of the ballistic missiles were intercepted amid a global shortage of Patriot interceptors.
Middle East France 24
Israel resumes military strikes on southern Lebanon as Rome hosts peace talks
Israel struck Tebnine after accusing Hezbollah of a "blatant" ceasefire violation, killing one and wounding a dozen, hours before a displacement order — the first serious escalation since the June truce, even as US-brokered talks continue in Rome.
Middle East Al Jazeera
Iran says Hormuz talks with Oman in 'final' stages as route approved
Tehran and Muscat have agreed coordinates for a new commercial shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz, with a joint statement in final drafting. Iran says the actual reopening still depends on the US lifting its naval blockade and halting strikes on Iranian infrastructure.
US Politics Al Jazeera
Abdul El-Sayed wins Michigan Democratic primary for Senate in blow to AIPAC
El-Sayed narrowly beat establishment-backed Rep. Haley Stevens despite a $65m outside-spending onslaught, a win for the party's progressive wing. He faces Republican Mike Rogers in November and would be the first Muslim US senator if elected.
