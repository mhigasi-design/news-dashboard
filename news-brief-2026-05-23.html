Iran · Diplomacy Wash. Post · CNBC
US-Iran Rome talks: Rubio says "slight progress" — round 5 ends without deal as both sides agree to continue
The fifth round of negotiations concluded in Rome without a breakthrough. Rubio said Friday the diplomatic path remains open but uncertainty about whether the ceasefire holds is "very high." Americas NPR · Foreign Policy
Trump threatens military action in Cuba after DOJ indicts Raúl Castro — "I'll be the one that does it"
The Trump administration indicted former Cuban President Raúl Castro over the 1996 downing of two civilian planes. Trump told reporters he may be the president to finally intervene militarily. Secretary Rubio, son of Cuban immigrants, called a diplomatic resolution with Cuba's current government "not high" in probability.
Tech · IPO CNBC · Bloomberg · Fortune
OpenAI files confidential IPO S-1 — $1T valuation target, September listing; twin mega-IPO summer with SpaceX
OpenAI has submitted a draft prospectus to regulators, targeting a $1 trillion-plus valuation and a public debut as early as September. The filing appears timed to rival SpaceX's June 11 listing — both companies are advised by the same syndicate (Goldman, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan). OpenAI's last private round valued it at $852B. A US jury cleared the company of Musk's lawsuit claims last week, removing a key IPO risk.
Ukraine · War CNN · Kyiv Independent
Ukraine drone strike kills 65 Russian cadets at pilot academy — first time since Aug 2024 Ukraine reclaims more land than Russia seizes
Ukraine targeted a drone pilot training camp in occupied eastern Ukraine, killing 65 cadets and an instructor. A separate strike killed and wounded nearly 100 at a Russian security HQ. Separately, analysts confirm Ukraine retook more territory than Russia captured last month for the first time since August 2024, despite Moscow still holding ~20% of Ukrainian land.
Global Health WHO · Al Jazeera
Ebola: WHO Emergency Committee issues temporary recommendations — outbreak spreads to M23 rebel territory, India-Africa summit cancelled
The IHR Emergency Committee met May 22 and issued temporary recommendations as the Bundibugyo strain outbreak hit 600+ suspected cases and 139 deaths across DRC and Uganda. A new cluster appeared in M23-controlled territory in North Kivu, complicating access. India postponed its Africa leaders summit citing the outbreak; no approved vaccine or treatment exists for the Bundibugyo strain.
