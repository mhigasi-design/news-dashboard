Conflict CNN · Bloomberg · Al Jazeera
Russia's largest Kyiv assault in months — 22 killed, 130+ sites hit ahead of NATO summit
74 missiles and 496 drones launched overnight; Ukraine air defences neutralised most drones but 25 ballistic missiles broke through, hitting 33 locations and destroying 64 apartments in one nine-storey block alone. Kyiv Mayor Klitschko declared Friday a day of mourning. Russia struck two days before Trump flies to Ankara for the July 7–8 NATO summit.
Middle East Al Jazeera · SCMP
IED kills 9, wounds 20 at Damascus café 40m from Palace of Justice
A device planted under a table detonated at 3:24 PM in a busy café in al-Marjah district as trials of former Assad officials proceed next door. No group has claimed responsibility. Damascus Governor Marwan said "bad actors" were seeking to destabilise Syria's transition; security services are hunting sleeper cells from the former regime.
Policy Al Jazeera · Bloomberg
South Africa: 900+ arrested as anti-migrant 'deadline day' marches turn violent
120 marches held nationwide — 108 peaceful, 12 requiring police — to mark an unofficial "deadline" for undocumented migrants to leave. One person shot dead in Johannesburg's Alexandra township; arrests for public violence, robbery and immigration violations spanned every province. President Ramaphosa called emergency talks with organisers and urged calm.
Vatican Al Jazeera · CNN
Pope Leo XIV excommunicates the SSPX — largest Roman Catholic schism in decades
The Society of St. Pius X consecrated four new bishops without papal approval, defying a personal appeal from Leo XIV hours earlier. Within 24 hours, the Vatican issued a full schism decree: all SSPX bishops, priests, and adherents excommunicated, their sacraments declared invalid. The traditionalist society counts an estimated 1.5 million faithful across 70 countries.
Iran · US Al Jazeera · CNN
Doha talks end with 'positive progress' — communication channel agreed as Khamenei state funeral looms
Qatar and Pakistan reported "positive progress" after two rounds of indirect US-Iran technical talks. Iran agreed to open a direct communication channel with Washington to resolve MoU disputes; the release of $6B in frozen assets was also discussed. Next session to be held "at the earliest possible time" after Iran's state funeral for Supreme Leader Khamenei (July 4–9, Mashhad).
Asia Al Jazeera · CNN · SCMP
8 monks killed in Thailand — 11-year-old boy steals truck, drives into Buddhist pilgrimage procession
A group of 35 monks from Mukdahan province was on a ritual dawn pilgrimage when an 11-year-old stole his parents' pick-up and drove into the procession. Five monks died at the scene; three more at hospital; 10–20 others injured. The boy is in custody. Thailand's road fatality rate remains among Asia's highest, re-igniting debate over underage driving enforcement.
