Russia-Ukraine BBC · Al Jazeera
Missiles hit Kyiv after Russia agrees to US call to resume Ukraine peace talks
Putin told US envoy Steve Witkoff in a 3-hour St Petersburg meeting that Moscow is willing in principle to resume trilateral Russia-US-Ukraine negotiations after 10 November. Zelensky wants talks "no later than early November," possibly in Abu Dhabi. Hours after the meeting, Russian missiles struck Kyiv, injuring six.
Published 21 Oct 2026
Corporate · Tech BBC · Al Jazeera
Meta fined $25bn for "lies" over user data privacy
A New Mexico jury found Meta misled Facebook users about data privacy in the Cambridge Analytica matter (43 million violations found); a judge ordered the company to pay $25bn — its largest-ever penalty, exceeding the FTC's 2019 $5bn fine. Meta says it will appeal.
Published 21 Oct 2026
Corporate BBC · Al Jazeera
Elon Musk confirms he is considering merging Tesla and SpaceX
Musk said on SpaceX's Q3 earnings call that SpaceX and Tesla have each formed special committees of independent directors to study a possible merger — reportedly the largest corporate tie-up in history, with a combined valuation above $3tn. Musk says he won't take part due to conflict of interest; no terms agreed, and a shareholder vote would be required.
Published 21 Oct 2026
Cybersecurity BBC · The Guardian
Websites taken down worldwide as critical cyber-security update issued
Two critical flaws in PHP — the language behind roughly 70% of websites — were found by security firm Wiz using AI tooling, prompting an emergency patch. Multiple governments (Finland, the Netherlands, Belgium and others) and organisations took sites offline as a precaution while patching; hackers were reportedly already exploiting the flaws.
