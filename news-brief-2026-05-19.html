Economy Bloomberg · Reuters
NextEra to buy Dominion Energy for $66.8B — world's largest regulated utility
All-stock deal creates a mega-utility by market value, driven by surging electricity demand from AI data centres. One of the largest transactions in US power sector history; positions NextEra to capitalise on AI infrastructure buildout.
Asia / Geopolitics NBC News · CNN
Taiwan pushes back on Trump's summit warnings — sovereignty "not for others to decide"
President Lai Ching-te reasserted that Taiwan's future is for Taiwanese to decide, after Trump's ambiguous Beijing comments raised alarm in Taipei. A $14B arms package remains in limbo; Taiwan signals it will not rely solely on US security assurances.
Tech / Asia Bloomberg · Tom's Hardware
Samsung faces 45,000-worker strike — AI chip supply chain at risk
Government-mediated talks between Samsung and its union continue as workers demand uncapped profit-sharing bonuses (est. ~$408K/employee). An 18-day walkout would disrupt global memory chip supply at a critical moment for AI infrastructure demand.
Geopolitics Euronews · MERICS
EU considers replacing China EV tariffs with voluntary export limits
Ahead of May 29 EU-China talks, Brussels is weighing minimum price agreements to replace 35.3% EV duties, while China retaliates with 62.4% levies on European pork and ongoing probes into cognac, dairy, and chemicals. Steel safeguards also under review.
US / Military NBC News · CBS News
Two Navy jets collide at Idaho air show — all 4 crew eject safely
Two EA-18G Growlers from VAQ-129 collided 2 miles northwest of Mountain Home AFB during Gunfighter Skies. All aviators ejected; 3 uninjured, 1 with minor injuries. Air show cancelled immediately; NTSB and Navy investigation underway.
Economy / Labor NBC News · ABC7
LIRR strike ends — MTA deal resumes service after first shutdown in 30 years
New York's MTA and Long Island Rail Road unions reached a tentative contract agreement, ending a historic strike that was the first in over three decades. The deal covers wage increases and healthcare premiums; full service resumed at noon Tuesday.
