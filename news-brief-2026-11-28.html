Conflict BBC · CBS News
Zelensky criticises "strange" Trump-Putin friendship in BBC interview
Zelensky told the BBC it was "strange" for Trump to call Putin "my friend," comparing it to calling Hitler a friend in WWII. He said the US isn't pushing Russia hard enough in peace talks and that delayed Patriot interceptor deliveries are "costing people's lives." Talks are expected to resume in December.
Policy BBC · SCMP
Canada's top court largely upholds Quebec ban on religious symbols for public servants
The Supreme Court mostly upheld Bill 21, which bars public sector workers from wearing the hijab, cross or other religious symbols, after challenges from Muslim, Sikh and Catholic teachers. PM Mark Carney said Ottawa would study the ruling's implications for minority rights nationwide.
Middle East Al Jazeera · BBC
Mecca pact allies condemn "heinous" Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia
Pakistan and Türkiye renewed their pledge to defend Saudi Arabia under the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement after a meeting in Islamabad. At least 26 people have been killed in Houthi drone and missile strikes on the kingdom since late September, 23 of them in attacks on airports.
Corporate Reuters · Al Jazeera
OpenAI countersues Apple over trade secrets as AI race heats up
OpenAI filed counterclaims accusing Apple of stealing trade secrets and blocking its hardware suppliers, alleging Apple's employment contracts "trap" staff. It follows Apple's own suit accusing OpenAI of poaching employees to access its technology — the latest flashpoint in the two companies' AI hardware rivalry.
Politics BBC · Al Jazeera
Spanish PM and rivals rally supporters in final pitch for votes
Pedro Sánchez told supporters "I cannot imagine us losing" at a closing rally in Valencia, accusing conservative rival Alberto Núñez Feijóo of planning a coalition with the far right. Feijóo's People's Party narrowly leads the polls ahead of Sunday's vote.
