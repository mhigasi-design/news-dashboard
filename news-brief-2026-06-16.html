📰 Top Stories 5
Middle East Al Jazeera · SCMP
Iran-US peace deal signed — Trump confirms "all done," Hormuz opens Friday
Trump and Vance electronically signed a preliminary MOU on June 16, with a formal ceremony set for Switzerland. Iran's FM confirmed the agreement covers ceasefire across all fronts, including Lebanon. The deal ends 107 days of conflict and triggers Hormuz reopening — WTI crude fell nearly 4% on the news.
Military SCMP · Al Jazeera
B-52 bomber crashes at Edwards AFB — all 8 crew presumed dead
A B-52 Stratofortress went down shortly after takeoff at Edwards Air Force Base in the Mojave Desert on June 15, leaving a towering black plume. The Air Force said "initial indications are that the crash was not survivable." The airfield was closed and a crash investigation board convened.
Middle East Al Jazeera · Reuters
Netanyahu: Israel will stay in Lebanon "as long as necessary" — 570 km² occupied
Despite the Iran-US ceasefire deal that addresses Lebanon, PM Netanyahu declared Israel would maintain its military buffer zone. His defiant position reportedly conflicts with Trump's expectations and threatens the fragile regional agreement reached this week.
Geopolitics SCMP · Reuters
EU's Kallas: China trained Russian soldiers in drone warfare — new sanctions
EU foreign affairs chief Kaja Kallas announced verified intelligence showing China trained approximately 200 Russian troops — primarily in drone operations — under a bilateral agreement signed in July 2025. The EU sanctioned several Chinese entities. Beijing denied the reports as inconsistent with its neutral stance.
Diplomacy SCMP · Al Jazeera
G7: Zelensky proposes meeting Putin in US after Kremlin snubs summit
At the G7 in France, Zelensky proposed a direct face-to-face meeting with Putin in the United States, saying he had discussed the venue with Trump and believed Putin would find it harder to refuse. The Kremlin ignored its G7 invitation and has yet to respond to the US venue proposal.
