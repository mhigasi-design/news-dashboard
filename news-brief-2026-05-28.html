Global Health Al Jazeera · SCMP
WHO chief warns DRC faces "catastrophic collision of disease and war" — Uganda seals border as Ebola tops 930 cases
WHO Director-General Tedros declared the DRC Ebola outbreak cannot be contained while conflict continues, with 930+ cases and 220+ suspected deaths since May 15. The rare Bundibugyo strain has no approved vaccine or treatment; 10 neighbouring nations face risk. Uganda closed its DRC border, permitting only authorised humanitarian teams; the DRC suspended all flights to Bunia. Tedros urged warring parties to declare an immediate ceasefire to allow health workers in.

Middle East Al Jazeera · SCMP
Trump rules out sanctions relief for Iran, threatens Oman over Strait of Hormuz control
Trump stated Iran will receive "no easing of sanctions or giving money" and demanded it surrender 440kg of enriched uranium. He threatened to "blow up" Oman — a US ally — if Hormuz is not kept open to all nations, a remark the White House did not walk back. Tehran called the US proposal a "complete fabrication" and released contradictory terms; fundamental disagreements over nuclear materials and the naval blockade persist.

Conflict Al Jazeera · SCMP
Israel orders Tyre evacuation and escalates Lebanon strikes — 31+ killed this week; Hamas armed wing chief killed in Gaza
Israel designated all Lebanese territory south of the Zahrani River a combat zone, ordering mass evacuation of Tyre. Strikes killed at least 3 people in southern Lebanon on May 27, following 31 deaths the prior day. Separately in Gaza, Israel killed Mohammad Odeh — the second Hamas armed wing chief in 13 days — along with his wife and son in a Rimal neighbourhood strike; Odeh may have been the last member of Hamas's senior military council.

Europe Al Jazeera · SCMP
Spanish police raid PM Sanchez's Socialist Party headquarters in National Court corruption probe
Officers entered PSOE headquarters under orders from Judge Santiago Pedraz, seeking documents related to party member Leire Diez, accused of paying to discredit an anti-corruption official and influence state prosecutors. The raid compounds mounting pressure on Sanchez, whose brother, wife, and predecessor Zapatero are all under separate investigations. Opposition leader Feijoo declared the government "stinks" of corruption and demanded early elections; Sanchez called the cases a right-wing smear.
