📰 Top Stories 5
Disaster BBC · Al Jazeera
Venezuela earthquakes kill 920 as families desperate for news
Twin 7.2 and 7.5 magnitude quakes struck near La Guaira on June 25–26, killing at least 920 and injuring 1,500+. Airport closed, state of emergency declared. Hundreds still feared trapped under rubble as international rescue teams begin arriving.
Middle East BBC · Al Jazeera
US conducts strikes on Iran after drone attack on Hormuz cargo ship
CENTCOM struck missile and drone storage facilities and coastal radar near Sirik after Iran's IRGC hit the Singapore-flagged Ever Lovely. Trump called it a "foolish violation" of the June 17 MOU. Iran retaliated against US Gulf bases. Both sides accuse the other of breaching the ceasefire; Al Jazeera notes the US response was "more restrained than past attacks."
Diplomacy BBC · Al Jazeera
Israel and Lebanon sign US-brokered framework agreement
Secretary Rubio announced the deal "establishes a clear process to restore Lebanon's sovereignty and dismantle Hezbollah's infrastructure." Lebanon PM Salam calls it a continuation of prior agreements. Israel still occupies ~20% of Lebanon; Netanyahu insists troops won't leave without Hezbollah disarmament. Hezbollah was not party to the talks.
Climate BBC · SCMP
Europe's deadly heatwave breaks German record, halts public events
Germany recorded a provisional all-time high of 41.3°C in Saarbrücken. UK hospitals logged the "highest-ever number of life-threatening emergency calls in a day." France saw 40°C+ with schools closed; heat deaths are being reported across the continent. The heatwave is also threatening tourism and wildlife.
Africa Al Jazeera · SCMP
Burkina Faso severs all diplomatic ties with France
Junta leader Captain Ibrahim Traore's government severed all diplomatic relations with France, accusing Paris of "neo-colonial ambitions" and supporting subversive networks. French nationals and cultural ties are to be preserved. The move is the latest in a rapid series of anti-French realignments across the Sahel, accelerating the shift toward Russia and China.
