Middle East Al Jazeera · AP
Israeli strikes destroy Gaza medicine warehouse, kill several
Israeli strikes overnight into Saturday hit central and northern Gaza, destroying a medicine storage warehouse next to Al-Aqsa Martyrs Hospital in Deir al-Balah and killing several people, Gaza's Health Ministry said, calling it a "heinous crime." Comes just days after Hamas agreed to a phased disarmament framework brokered by the Board of Peace.
Europe Al Jazeera · AP
Spain installs sea barrier at Ceuta after 67 die in mass crossing
Spain's Civil Guard began installing a 500-metre floating containment barrier at the Tarajal breakwater between Ceuta and Morocco, a sharp escalation after a mass migrant crossing left at least 67 people dead — beyond the emergency Madrid had already declared days earlier.
Climate Al Jazeera · AP
Gale-force winds fan new Greek wildfires as France's blazes ease
Winds up to 130km/h drove wildfires through the Porto Germeno area near Athens on Saturday, forcing sea evacuations, even as major fires that had displaced hundreds of thousands across France and Spain showed signs of coming under control.
Asia-Pacific AP · SCMP
China holds military drills near disputed Scarborough Shoal
PLA naval and coast guard forces conducted joint drills near Scarborough Shoal (Huangyan Dao), including deployment of supersonic anti-ship missiles, days after the Philippines submitted a UN bid to extend its seabed rights over the contested reef.
