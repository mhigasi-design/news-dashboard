📰 Top Stories 5
Red Sea Al Jazeera · Bloomberg
Houthis declare naval blockade of Saudi Arabia — all ships warned away from Saudi ports
Iran-aligned Yemeni group announced an effective maritime embargo, warning vessels may be "targeted" if they call at Saudi ports. Bab al-Mandeb handles ~7% of global oil (7.4M bbl/day). Combined with the already-closed Strait of Hormuz, analysts warn up to 25% of world energy supply could be restricted — pushing oil above $100/bbl.
Iran War Al Jazeera · CNN
US-Iran war enters 10th consecutive night — Iran hits Kuwait desalination plant and power infrastructure
Following ten straight nights of US bombing, Iran struck a desalination facility and power plants in Kuwait, and fired on targets in Bahrain and Jordan. Trump claims Iran is "desperate" to negotiate while threatening to strike the Pickaxe Mountain nuclear site. Pentagon says the war has cost $37.5B so far; ~100 US personnel injured.
Trade Al Jazeera · Bloomberg · CNN
Trump imposes 50% tariffs on Canada under 1930 law — targets $20B in goods; Carney pushes back but agrees to speed up talks
Using Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 — not invoked in nearly a century — Trump announced 50% tariffs on Canadian wine, dairy, cement, furniture and textiles, effective August 19. PM Carney called it a violation of the trilateral trade agreement. Both leaders agreed to accelerate USMCA negotiations ahead of the deadline.
Politics Al Jazeera · Bloomberg
India's "Cockroach" student movement halts marches after Delhi police crack down on 10,000-strong rally
Over 10,000 students marched on parliament demanding the education minister's resignation over exam paper leaks affecting 2.2M medical applicants, linked to at least 12 student suicides. Police fired tear gas and used batons; 150+ protesters injured, 70 detained. Rahul Gandhi staged a separate protest at PM Modi's residence.
Policy SCMP
France becomes first EU country to ban social media for under-15s — Macron targets September rollout
France's National Assembly voted 279-81 to prohibit social media access for children under 15, with TikTok among platforms targeted. A separate clause bans mobile phones in high schools starting next school year. The Constitutional Council must review the legislation first; Macron says the government "aims to enforce it by September."
