📰 Top Stories 4
Middle East BBC · Guardian · AP · Al Jazeera
US strikes Iranian launchers on Larak island amid Strait of Hormuz tensions
US forces struck two Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island — the first known US strike on Iran since late July — after the IRGC was observed prepping rockets to fire sea mines into the strait. Iran says the attack killed and wounded soldiers and civilians and has vowed "response and punishment"; Reuters later reported an Iranian retaliatory strike on US forces in Jordan was intercepted with no impact. The war has now passed its six-month mark.
Cyprus BBC · Guardian · Al Jazeera
At least eight dead and 18 missing after ferry capsizes off northern Cyprus
A catamaran ferry carrying nearly 270 passengers and crew capsized and sank to a depth of 514 metres off Kyrenia in Turkish-occupied northern Cyprus while en route to Taşucu, Turkey. Authorities say 237 people have been rescued; search teams are racing to reach anyone still trapped inside the wreck.
Europe BBC · Guardian · AP
Iceland rejects EU accession talks after divisive referendum campaign
Icelanders voted 52.8% to 47.2% against reopening EU accession talks first suspended in 2013, on turnout of 82.5%. The closely fought campaign played out against Donald Trump's repeated threats toward neighbouring Greenland, with "yes" campaigners arguing EU membership would offer greater protection. PM Kristrún Frostadóttir said the vote "raised the debate in Iceland to a higher level."
South Asia AP · Guardian
Nepal warns of possible fresh flooding as communities grapple with devastation
Nepal's disaster agency says the death toll from last week's glacier-collapse flooding has climbed to 788 dead with 2,502 missing, plus 16 dead and 546 missing on the Chinese side — a combined toll of over 800. Authorities issued fresh flood warnings Monday for Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading districts after China reported rising water levels upstream in the Bhotekoshi river.
