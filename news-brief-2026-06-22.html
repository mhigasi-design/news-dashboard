Middle East Al Jazeera · CNN
Iran's IRGC shuts Hormuz over Lebanon — Vance leads Switzerland talks as Trump threatens fresh strikes
Iran cited US failure to enforce Lebanon ceasefire as justification for declaring Hormuz closed. VP Vance led an 80-minute session with Iran's Ghalibaf in Burgenstock, with Pakistan and Qatar mediating. Despite the closure declaration, the US military confirmed 55 merchant ships transited Saturday carrying 17 million barrels.
Conflict Al Jazeera · CNN
100+ Israeli strikes on Lebanon since midnight — 4,106 killed since March 2; Netanyahu vows permanent buffer zone
Lebanon's Health Ministry reported 83 killed on Friday alone, with over 100 airstrikes in southern Lebanon since midnight Saturday. PM Netanyahu stated Israel "established a security zone in Lebanon and shall keep it as long as necessary." Lebanon-Israel talks are next scheduled in Washington June 23–25.
Latin America Bloomberg · CNN · Al Jazeera
Colombia's tightest-ever election: De la Espriella defeats leftist Cepeda 49.7%–48.7%
Trump-backed conservative Abelardo "El Tigre" de la Espriella edged out Petro ally Iván Cepeda by just one percentage point with 99.7% of votes counted. The result ends four years of Petro's leftist government and signals a sharp realignment with Washington on drug policy and security.
World Cup Al Jazeera · CNN
Spain 4–0 Saudi Arabia; Japan 4–0 Tunisia in WC game #1,000; Curacao earns historic first point
Lamine Yamal netted his first World Cup goal as Spain bounced back from their opening draw to dominate Group H. Japan thrashed Tunisia in what FIFA confirmed was the competition's 1,000th-ever match. Curacao goalkeeper Eloy Room saved a late penalty to earn the Caribbean nation's first World Cup point in a 0–0 draw with Ecuador.
