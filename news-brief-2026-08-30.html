Russia-Ukraine War AP · Al Jazeera
Russian strike on a Ukrainian warehouse kills 37 in the war's deadliest attack this year
A Russian drone hit a munitions warehouse in Myla, Bucha district, sparking explosions that damaged 50+ homes and killed 34 residents of a care facility. It was the second and deadliest of three straight days of near-constant strikes on the Kyiv region.
US Policy AP · Al Jazeera
Trump cannot deport students for criticizing Israel, judge rules
Federal judge Noël Wise ruled the Trump administration unconstitutionally targeted noncitizens for deportation over pro-Palestinian speech, siding with The Stanford Daily. The ruling builds on a similar Boston federal court finding from last year.
Europe AP · Al Jazeera
Trump's Greenland threats cast a shadow on Iceland's vote on EU membership talks
About 265,000 Icelanders voted Saturday on whether to resume long-stalled EU accession talks, first suspended in 2013. Polling showed the race too close to call, with Arctic security and cost-of-living concerns driving the debate. Result expected Sunday.
Haiti AP · Al Jazeera
Violent Haitian gang releases women and children after mass kidnapping and deadly attack
UNICEF says a gang freed 6 children and 7 women taken in last week's Kenscoff attack, which killed at least 47 and saw more than 50 people kidnapped. It's unclear if more hostages remain held or whether those released need medical care.
