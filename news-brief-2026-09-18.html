Politics The Guardian · BBC
Sweden's PM resigns after centre-left bloc wins slim majority in election
Ulf Kristersson stood down after four years, clearing the way for Social Democrats leader Magdalena Andersson to form a government. Left-leaning parties took 176 seats — a narrow 3-seat majority over Kristersson's Tidö coalition. The far-right Sweden Democrats lost support for the first time since their founding.
Middle East The Guardian · BBC
Iran school bombing: grounds to believe US was behind atrocity, UN finds
A UN fact-finding mission found reasonable grounds the US was behind February strikes on a school and sports complex in Iran that killed 156 civilians, including 120 children, calling them war crimes. The Pentagon has not acknowledged responsibility; its own internal probe reportedly concluded months ago.
Europe The Guardian · BBC
Canada's Carney embraces EU associate membership proposal
Addressing the European Parliament, Carney welcomed becoming the EU's first "associate member," citing overwhelming Canadian public support for closer ties. Trump had called the idea "laughable" hours earlier and threatened "very serious tariffs" on Europe if it proceeds.
