France passes landmark assisted-dying law — 291 to 241 in National Assembly
The French parliament approved historic end-of-life legislation in a 291–241 vote. The law permits French citizens and legal residents with incurable, life-threatening illnesses in terminal stages — with full decision-making capacity — to request a lethal substance. The Constitutional Council must still sign off before enactment.
