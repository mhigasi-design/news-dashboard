US Politics BBC · AP
Supreme Court clears way for Trump to keep building White House ballroom
In a 5-4 ruling, the Supreme Court granted the Trump administration's emergency request to block a lower-court order that had halted construction of the $400M ballroom, built on the demolished East Wing. The conservative majority found the challenger likely lacks standing — it did not rule on the project's legality. Chief Justice Roberts, joined by the three liberal justices, dissented, writing the construction "is likely unlawful" since Congress hasn't authorized it.
Middle East Al Jazeera · AP
UAE intercepts Iranian drone as US-Iran hostilities flare
The UAE Defense Ministry says it intercepted an Iranian drone approaching over its territorial waters, with no reported damage; the Foreign Ministry called it a "dangerous escalation" and a violation of UAE sovereignty. It follows the first major US-Iran military exchange in weeks: a US strike on Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz, and an Iranian missile response toward US sites in Jordan, intercepted.
Geopolitics AP · Al Jazeera
Xi, Putin and Modi meet at Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit in Bishkek
Leaders of China, Russia, India and other SCO member states — plus Iran's president, Pakistan's PM and Turkey's Erdoğan as guests — gathered in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan for the two-day summit marking the SCO's 25th anniversary. Putin and Xi used the sidelines to reaffirm their "strategic alliance" amid what they called an "unpredictable" world; Modi separately urged Putin toward a peaceful resolution in Ukraine.
Business BBC · AP
Shein makes long-delayed Hong Kong stock market debut, valued near $27B
Fast-fashion retailer Shein begins trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange after a years-long, repeatedly delayed quest to list, having previously targeted New York and London. The IPO raises up to ~$1.76B via ~280 million shares, valuing the company at up to $27B — a fraction of its ~$100B peak valuation in 2022, pressured by US/EU moves to end de-minimis tariff exemptions on small parcel imports.
