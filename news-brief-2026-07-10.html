Health Crisis SCMP · Africa CDC · WHO
Ebola DRC outbreak: "fastest growing ever" — 600 dead, 1,759 confirmed cases
Cases doubling every 28 days; within six weeks this outbreak had 1,596 cases vs 994 in the comparable period of the 2014 West Africa epidemic. Africa CDC warns it could surpass that outbreak's total. $1.4B funding needed. "The virus is still ahead of our response."
Geopolitics Al Jazeera · Reuters · BBC
Palestinian elections set for November 28 — first vote in 20 years
President Abbas issued a decree Thursday for legislative elections across West Bank, East Jerusalem and Gaza. The last vote was in 2006, which Hamas won, triggering its split from the PA. France, Saudi Arabia and others have pushed Abbas to demonstrate democratic legitimacy. Major obstacles: Israeli access to East Jerusalem and Gaza's decimated infrastructure.
Conflict · Gaza Al Jazeera · BBC
Gaza: 6 killed in Israeli strikes despite ceasefire — including aid worker
A World Central Kitchen aid driver was killed when an Israeli strike hit a food delivery vehicle. Four others died in drone strikes on Nuseirat camp and Khan Younis. Since the Oct 2025 ceasefire began, at least 1,092 Palestinians have been killed. Israel now occupies ~80% of Gaza beyond the agreed Yellow Line boundary.
Middle East SCMP · Al Jazeera
Khamenei buried in Mashhad as millions chant for revenge against US and Israel
The Supreme Leader's coffin was transported by helicopter to the Shrine of Imam Reza for burial, escorted by a fighter jet. Khamenei was killed in an Israeli strike in late February 2026, triggering the US-Israeli military campaign against Iran. Despite a peace accord signed in June, new hostilities resumed this week.
